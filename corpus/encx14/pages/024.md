# 24 Hall array and front end

## 24.1 Hall array

Four Hall plates are arranged on a circle of 1.0 mm radius at 0°, 90°,
180° and 270°. Opposite plates are subtracted to cancel any uniform
external field along the package normal. The difference signals are
proportional to Bx and By of the magnet field.

## 24.2 Common-mode field rejection

A uniform external field (for example the field of a nearby motor) is
rejected by the differential arrangement with a ratio of about 40 dB. A
non-uniform stray field is not rejected and appears as an angle error (page
22).

## 24.3 Automatic gain control

The AGC adjusts the front-end gain so that the amplitude of the Bx/By
signals at the ADC input is constant. The AGC value (0 to 255) is readable
in register AGC (0x1A, page 35). A low AGC value means a strong field; a
high value means a weak field.

The AGC loop time constant is 10 ms. The AGC does not affect the angle
directly, but a fast change of air gap (axial shock) can cause a short
angle disturbance while the AGC settles.

## 24.4 Front-end noise

The input-referred noise of the front end is 2.1 µT RMS in the 4 kHz
bandwidth of the ADC. Together with the Hall plate noise and quantisation,
this gives the 12 LSB RMS total output noise at FILT = 0 (page 26).

## 24.5 Front-end saturation

Above 120 mT the front end saturates. MAGH is set. The angle is invalid
while MAGH is set and must not be used for control.
