## 8.3 Evaluation board

The ENCX14-EVB carries the encoder, a magnet holder with a 6 mm x 2.5 mm
NdFeB magnet, a 3.3 V regulator, I²C pull-ups (2.2 kOhm) and a 2.54 mm
header for a host. Jumper J1 selects the I²C address. Jumper J2 inserts a
10 Ohm resistor in series with VDD so that brown-out behaviour can be
reproduced with a bench supply.
