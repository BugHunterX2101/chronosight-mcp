## 6.1 Data path

The four Hall elements sense the two orthogonal field components of the
rotating magnet. The front end amplifies them with a gain set by the
automatic gain control (AGC, page 35). The ADC samples both channels at
8 kHz. The CORDIC engine converts each sample pair into an angle.

The FILT filter (page 31) is applied to the angle stream at the full 8 kHz
rate, before the result is written to the RAW register. ZPOS, MPOS, DIR and
HYST are applied after the filter, and the result goes to the ANGLE register.

## 6.2 Reset controller

Every reset source drives the same internal reset line. The reset line
clears the volatile registers, including CONF2, regardless of the source.
This is the root of errata E-3 (page 58).
