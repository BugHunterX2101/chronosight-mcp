# 52 Magnet and field faults

## 52.1 Field flags

  Condition                   Flags                  Angle valid
  Field 30 mT to 90 mT        MD = 1                 yes
  Field below 30 mT           MAGL = 1, MD = 1       no
  Field above 90 mT           MAGH = 1, MD = 1       no
  Field below 20 mT           MAGL = 1, MD = 0       no
  Field above 120 mT          MAGH = 1, MD = 0       no

MAGL, MAGH and MD are not sticky; they reflect the field at the time of
the read. OVF is sticky and cleared by a STATUS read.

## 52.2 Typical causes

  - Air gap too large (field too weak): see the air gap table on page 20.
  - Air gap too small or wrong magnet grade (field too strong).
  - Magnet loosened or cracked: MAG changes over time, AGC drifts.
  - External field from a nearby motor: AGC and MAG change with motor
    current.

## 52.3 Host response

When the angle is not valid, hold the last motor command or bring the
joint to a safe state. Do not use the angle (page 40). Log MAG and AGC
with the event.

## 52.4 Trend monitoring

AGC moves slowly as the magnet ages and with temperature (page 35). A
change of more than 20 % from the value recorded at production test
indicates a mechanical problem.

## 52.5 Power-up glitch on rev 0xA1

On rev 0xA1 silicon MAGH can be set for up to 1 ms after a power-on reset
even with a correct field (errata E-2, page 57). Wait 2 ms after reset
before acting on MAGH.
