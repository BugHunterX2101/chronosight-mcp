# 18 Soldering and handling

## 18.1 Reflow profile

The device is qualified for lead-free reflow according to the following
profile:

  Stage                              Value
  Preheat, 150 to 200 °C             60 to 120 s
  Time above 217 °C                  60 to 150 s
  Peak temperature                   260 °C, max 10 s within 5 °C of peak
  Ramp-up rate                       3 °C/s max
  Ramp-down rate                     6 °C/s max
  Maximum number of reflows          3

## 18.2 Moisture sensitivity

The device is MSL 3. After opening the dry pack, mount within 168 hours at
30 °C and 60 % RH or less, or bake at 125 °C for 24 hours.

## 18.3 Hand soldering

Hand soldering is allowed with a soldering iron at up to 350 °C for 3 s per
lead. Do not touch the top of the package with the iron; the mould compound
above the Hall array is thin.

## 18.4 Cleaning

Use no-clean flux where possible. If cleaning is needed, use isopropyl
alcohol. Do not use ultrasonic cleaning; it can crack the die attach.

## 18.5 Handling the magnet

NdFeB magnets are brittle and attract each other strongly. Store magnets
apart from encoders and from each other. Do not machine NdFeB magnets.

## 18.6 OTP and soldering

OTP contents are not affected by reflow. Program OTP after assembly (page
46), because the zero position depends on the final mechanical mounting.
