# 9 Absolute maximum ratings

Stresses beyond the values in table 9-1 may cause permanent damage.
Operation at these values is not implied. Exposure to absolute maximum
conditions for long periods may affect reliability.

Table 9-1. Absolute maximum ratings.

  Parameter                          Min      Max      Unit
  Supply voltage VDD                 -0.3     4.0      V
  Voltage on SDA, SCL, ADDR          -0.3     VDD+0.3  V
  Voltage on TEST                    -0.3     VDD+0.3  V
  Input current, any pin             -10      10       mA
  Storage temperature                -55      150      °C
  Junction temperature                        150      °C
  Magnetic flux density                       unlimited
  ESD, human body model (HBM)                 2000     V
  ESD, charged device model (CDM)             500      V
  Moisture sensitivity level                  MSL 3

## 9.1 Notes

1. The device survives any static magnetic field. The angle output is only
   valid between 30 mT and 90 mT (page 20).
2. TEST must be tied to GND in the application. Leaving it open can enter a
   factory test mode in which the I²C interface does not respond.
3. Exceeding 4.0 V on VDD can damage the OTP memory. OTP contents are not
   guaranteed after an overvoltage event.
4. A supply ramp faster than 1 V/µs can trigger more than one power-on
   reset. This is harmless; the last reset wins.
