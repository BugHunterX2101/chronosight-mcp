# 23 Signal chain overview

The signal chain converts the magnetic field into a 14-bit angle in five
stages:

  1. Hall array. Four Hall plates measure the two field components Bx, By.
  2. Front end. Differential amplifiers with automatic gain control.
  3. ADC. Two 12-bit sigma-delta converters sampled at 8 kHz.
  4. CORDIC. Computes atan2(By, Bx) to 14 bits once per sample.
  5. FILT. A first-order IIR low-pass filter on the angle (page 31).

After the filter, the output stage applies ZPOS, MPOS, DIR and HYST and
updates ANGLE and RAW once every 125 µs.

## 23.1 Angle wrap handling in the filter

The filter works on the unwrapped angle, so a transition from code 16383 to
code 0 does not produce a filtered spike. The output is wrapped again to 14
bits after the filter.

## 23.2 Where the noise comes from

Most of the output noise comes from the Hall plates and the front end
(thermal and 1/f noise) and from ADC quantisation. CORDIC rounding adds less
than 0.3 LSB RMS. Page 26 gives the breakdown.

## 23.3 Where the delay comes from

  Stage                      Delay
  ADC conversion             40 µs
  CORDIC                     20 µs
  FILT (when enabled)        tau = 2^n / 8000 s, see table 31-2
  Register update            0 to 125 µs (sampling phase)
  I²C transfer               see page 14
  Host scheduling            depends on the host (page 12)

The total delay seen by a typical 1 kHz host is 2 ms (page 12), plus the
FILT time constant if the filter is enabled.
