# 10 Recommended operating conditions

Table 10-1. Recommended operating conditions.

  Parameter                          Min     Typ     Max     Unit
  Supply voltage VDD                 3.0     3.3     3.6     V
  Supply current, active                     6.5     9.0     mA
  Supply current, low-power mode             1.2     1.8     mA
  Operating ambient temperature      -40             125     °C
  Magnetic flux density              30      60      90      mT
  Air gap (6 mm x 2.5 mm magnet)     0.5     1.5     3.0     mm
  Rotational speed                           0       30000   rpm
  I²C clock frequency                0               1000    kHz
  Supply ripple, 1 kHz to 1 MHz                      50      mV pp

## 10.1 Supply decoupling

Place a 100 nF ceramic capacitor within 2 mm of the VDD pin and a 4.7 µF
capacitor within 10 mm. The device draws current pulses at the 8 kHz
sampling rate. Without the 4.7 µF capacitor these pulses can modulate the
supply enough to add 1-2 LSB of noise.

## 10.2 Shared supplies

When the encoder shares a 3.3 V rail with a motor driver or a radio,
consider a separate LDO or an RC filter (10 Ohm, 10 µF) on VDD. Supply dips
below VBOR = 2.6 V cause a brown-out reset (page 11). The brown-out reset
clears CONF2 (page 58).

## 10.3 Rotational speed

The angle output is valid up to 30,000 rpm. At high speed the output lags
the true angle by the internal processing delay plus the FILT time constant
(page 12 and page 31). The lag is a pure function of speed and can be
compensated by the host if needed.
