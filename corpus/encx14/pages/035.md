# 35 AGC and MAG registers

## 35.1 AGC (0x1A, R)

  Bits   Field    Description
  7:0    AGC      Automatic gain control value, 0 (strong) to 255 (weak)

The AGC value is a convenient indicator of the air gap. Table 20-1 lists
typical values for the reference magnet. A slowly rising AGC value over the
life of a product indicates a growing air gap or a weakening magnet.

## 35.2 MAG (0x1B-0x1C, R)

  Register   Bits   Field         Description
  MAG_H      3:0    MAG[11:8]     Field magnitude after AGC, high bits
  MAG_L      7:0    MAG[7:0]      Field magnitude after AGC, low bits

MAG is the magnitude sqrt(Bx^2 + By^2) after the AGC. With the AGC in its
range, MAG is constant at about 2048. MAG that varies by more than ±10 %
during one revolution indicates misalignment (page 21).

## 35.3 Using AGC and MAG in production test

  Test                          Pass criterion
  AGC at nominal air gap        40 to 120 (reference magnet)
  MAG variation per revolution  within ±10 %
  STATUS.MD                     1
  STATUS.MAGL, STATUS.MAGH      0

## 35.4 AGC and noise

A high AGC value (weak field) means more front-end gain and more noise. The
noise multipliers on page 26 apply. If FILT is chosen from table 31-2 for a
60 mT design, check the noise again at the weakest expected field.
