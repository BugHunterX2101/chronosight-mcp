## 55.3 Magnet ageing

The device itself does not age measurably. NdFeB magnets lose about 0.1 %
of remanence per 1000 h at 100 °C. Over a product life this changes the
field by a few percent, which the AGC compensates (page 35, page 52).

## 55.4 Reliability and resets

Brown-out resets do not damage the device and do not count against its
reliability. They affect the application through the loss of CONF2
(page 58), not through wear.
