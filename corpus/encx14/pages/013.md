# 13 I²C DC characteristics and address selection

Table 13-1. I²C DC characteristics (VDD = 3.0 to 3.6 V).

  Parameter                          Min        Max        Unit
  Input low voltage VIL              -0.3       0.3 VDD    V
  Input high voltage VIH             0.7 VDD    VDD+0.3    V
  Input hysteresis                   0.05 VDD              V
  Output low voltage, 3 mA sink                 0.4        V
  Output low voltage, 20 mA sink                0.4        V    (Fm+)
  Input leakage current              -1         1          µA
  Pin capacitance                               10         pF

## 13.1 Pull-up resistors

SDA and SCL are open-drain. Choose pull-ups for the bus capacitance and
speed:

  Mode               Max clock   Typical pull-up   Max bus capacitance
  Standard mode      100 kHz     4.7 kOhm          400 pF
  Fast mode          400 kHz     2.2 kOhm          400 pF
  Fast-mode plus     1 MHz       1.0 kOhm          550 pF

## 13.2 Address selection

The 7-bit I²C address is set by the ADDR pin at reset:

  ADDR pin     7-bit address    Write byte   Read byte
  GND          0x4C             0x98         0x99
  VDD          0x4D             0x9A         0x9B

The ADDR pin is sampled on every reset, including brown-out resets. Do not
drive ADDR from a GPIO that may change state while the device is running:
a brown-out would then move the device to the other address.

## 13.3 Bus voltage

The I²C pins are not 5 V tolerant. A 5 V host needs a level shifter.
