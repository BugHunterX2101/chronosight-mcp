# 17 PCB layout guidance

## 17.1 Placement

  - Place the decoupling capacitors on the same layer as the encoder, as
    close to pins 1 and 2 as possible.
  - Route SDA and SCL as a pair, away from motor phase traces.
  - Keep current-carrying traces at least 5 mm from the package. A trace
    carrying 1 A at 5 mm produces about 40 µT, which is small compared with
    the 30 mT minimum signal, but motor phase currents of 10 A or more are
    not.

## 17.2 Ground

Connect GND to a solid ground plane. Do not route the motor return current
under the encoder.

## 17.3 Supply

If the board carries a motor driver, give the encoder its own supply branch
from the regulator, with an RC filter (10 Ohm, 10 µF). This reduces the
probability of a brown-out reset when the motor starts (page 53). It does
not remove that probability, so firmware must still handle STATUS.RST (page
45).

## 17.4 Connectors and cables

When the encoder board is connected to the host by a cable longer than
30 cm, use fast mode (400 kHz) rather than fast-mode plus, add 100 Ohm
series resistors on SDA and SCL at the encoder side, and keep VDD and GND in
the same cable as the bus. Long cables raise the chance of supply dips at
the encoder.

## 17.5 Magnet keep-out

Keep ferromagnetic parts (steel screws, shields) at least 5 mm away from the
magnet. Ferromagnetic parts distort the field and add an angle error that
repeats once per revolution (page 27).
