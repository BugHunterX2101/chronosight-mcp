## 56.3 Handling

Handle the device at an ESD-protected workstation (page 4). The SDA, SCL
and ADDR pins are not protected against system-level ESD; add external
protection if the I²C bus leaves the board.

## 56.4 Supply transients

The device tolerates supply transients within the absolute maximum ratings
(page 9). Transients that pull VDD below VBOR cause a brown-out reset
(page 11); they do not damage the device.
