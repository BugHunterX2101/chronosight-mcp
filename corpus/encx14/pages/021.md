# 21 Mechanical alignment

## 21.1 Lateral displacement

The magnet axis should coincide with the centre of the Hall array (page
16). Lateral displacement causes an angle error that repeats once per
revolution.

  Lateral displacement    Peak angle error (reference magnet, 1.5 mm gap)
  0.10 mm                 0.10°
  0.25 mm                 0.28°
  0.50 mm                 0.62°
  1.00 mm                 1.50°

## 21.2 Tilt

Tilt of the magnet relative to the package surface causes an angle error
that repeats twice per revolution.

  Tilt      Peak angle error
  1°        0.05°
  2°        0.12°
  5°        0.35°

## 21.3 Run-out

Shaft run-out moves the magnet in a circle. The error is the same as a
lateral displacement equal to the run-out radius, but its phase rotates with
the shaft.

## 21.4 Alignment procedure

1. Mount the encoder board and the magnet holder.
2. Rotate the shaft slowly through one revolution while reading ANGLE.
3. Compare ANGLE with a reference (a second encoder or an optical index).
4. Fit a sine at once and twice the rotation frequency to the difference.
5. If the once-per-revolution amplitude exceeds the budget, re-centre the
   board.

## 21.5 Effect on servo loops

Alignment errors are smooth and repeat with the shaft angle. They appear as
a slow position error, not as noise. They are not reduced by FILT (page 31).
