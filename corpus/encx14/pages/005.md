# 5 Features

  - 14-bit absolute angle output, 16384 codes per revolution
  - 1 LSB = 0.02197° (383.5 µrad)
  - Contactless: on-axis measurement of a diametric magnet, 30 to 90 mT
  - Internal sampling at 8 kHz with a CORDIC angle engine
  - Programmable first-order digital noise filter, FILT[2:0], with eight
    settings from "off" to a 16 ms time constant (page 31)
  - Output noise 12 LSB RMS with the filter off, down to 0.75 LSB RMS with
    the strongest setting (table 31-2)
  - I²C interface, standard mode, fast mode and fast-mode plus (up to 1 MHz)
  - Two selectable I²C addresses, 0x4C or 0x4D (ADDR pin)
  - Programmable zero position and angular range (ZPOS, MPOS)
  - Selectable direction of rotation (DIR bit)
  - Output hysteresis of 0, 1, 2 or 3 LSB (HYST field)
  - Magnet diagnostics: field too weak (MAGL), field too strong (MAGH),
    magnet detected (MD), automatic gain control readout (AGC)
  - Sticky reset flag (STATUS.RST) that reports any reset since the last
    STATUS read
  - Internal watchdog that resets the device if the oscillator stalls
  - Supply 3.0 V to 3.6 V, typical current 6.5 mA
  - Power-on reset (VPOR 2.8 V) and brown-out reset (VBOR 2.6 V)
  - Operating temperature -40 °C to +125 °C
  - SOIC-8 package, 4.9 mm x 3.9 mm
  - One-time programmable (OTP) memory for ZPOS, MPOS and CONF1

## 5.1 What the device does not do

  - It does not store CONF2 in OTP. The FILT, DIR, HYST and WD fields are
    volatile and return to their defaults after every reset (page 44).
  - It does not provide a velocity output. Velocity must be estimated by the
    host from successive angle reads (page 50).
  - It does not provide an incremental (ABI) or PWM output. All data is read
    over I²C.
