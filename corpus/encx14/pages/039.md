# 39 Read transaction

## 39.1 Sequence

  S | 0x98 | A | ptr | A | Sr | 0x99 | A | data0 | A | data1 | A | ... | NA | P

The host acknowledges every byte except the last, which it NACKs before the
STOP.

## 39.2 Example: read CHIP_ID

  S | 0x98 | A | 0x00 | A | Sr | 0x99 | A | 0x14 | NA | P

## 39.3 Example: read ANGLE

  S | 0x98 | A | 0x0C | A | Sr | 0x99 | A | ANGLE_H | A | ANGLE_L | NA | P

The two bytes are latched together (page 34).

## 39.4 Reading STATUS clears flags

Any read transaction that includes address 0x0B clears STATUS.RST and
STATUS.OVF after the byte has been transmitted (page 33). This includes
burst reads that start below 0x0B and run through it.

## 39.5 Read latency

The data returned are the values latched at the start of the read. The
latency seen by a typical host is given on page 12.
