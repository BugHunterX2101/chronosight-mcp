# 20 Air gap and field strength

## 20.1 Field versus air gap

Table 20-1 gives the field at the Hall array for the reference magnet
(NdFeB N35, 6 mm x 2.5 mm) as a function of the mechanical air gap tAIR,
measured from the top of the package to the face of the magnet.

Table 20-1. Field versus air gap, reference magnet, 25 °C.

  tAIR (mm)    Field (mT)   AGC (typ)   Status flags
  0.25         121          0           MAGH
  0.50         98           12          MAGH (marginal)
  0.75         88           24          -
  1.00         79           41          -
  1.50         62           78          -
  2.00         48           112         -
  2.50         37           151         -
  3.00         30           186         MAGL (marginal)
  3.50         24           220         MAGL
  4.00         19           255         MAGL

Rev 1.1 corrected the tAIR maximum from 3.5 mm to 3.0 mm.

## 20.2 Choosing the air gap

Choose a nominal air gap in the middle of the valid field range so that
tolerances and temperature drift keep the field within 30 to 90 mT. For the
reference magnet, 1.5 mm is the recommended nominal value.

## 20.3 Field and noise

The output noise in table 31-2 is specified at 60 mT. At 30 mT add 20 %.
At 90 mT subtract 5 %. These factors apply to all FILT settings.

## 20.4 Axial play

Axial play of the shaft changes the air gap. A play of ±0.2 mm around a
1.5 mm nominal gap changes the field by about ±7 mT, which is well within
the AGC range and has no effect on the angle.
