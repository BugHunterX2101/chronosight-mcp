# 19 Magnet selection

## 19.1 Magnet type

Use a diametrically magnetised cylindrical magnet. The north and south
poles are on opposite halves of the cylinder, not on its end faces. An
axially magnetised magnet does not work with this device.

## 19.2 Recommended magnets

Table 19-1. Recommended magnets.

  Material      Diameter   Height   Air gap range   Notes
  NdFeB N35     6 mm       2.5 mm   0.5 to 3.0 mm   Reference magnet
  NdFeB N35     8 mm       3.0 mm   1.0 to 4.0 mm   Larger air gap
  NdFeB N35     4 mm       2.0 mm   0.5 to 1.8 mm   Small joints
  SmCo 2:17     6 mm       2.5 mm   0.5 to 2.4 mm   High temperature

## 19.3 Temperature

The remanence of NdFeB falls by about 0.12 % per °C. Between 25 °C and
125 °C the field falls by about 12 %. The AGC (page 35) compensates for the
loss automatically as long as the field stays above 30 mT. Check the field
at the highest operating temperature and largest air gap.

## 19.4 Magnet holder

Fix the magnet in a non-ferromagnetic holder (brass, aluminium, plastic).
Use an adhesive that keeps the magnet centred to within 0.1 mm. A magnet
that shifts in its holder causes a slowly growing angle error.
