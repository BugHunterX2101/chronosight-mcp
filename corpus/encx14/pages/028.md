## 28.1 Volatile and non-volatile registers

CONF1, ZPOS and MPOS have OTP shadows: after a reset they are reloaded from
OTP. CONF2 has no OTP shadow and returns to 0x00 after every reset. STATUS
returns to 0x01 (RST set) after every reset.

## 28.2 Reserved addresses

Writes to reserved addresses are ignored. Reads return 0x00, except 0x10
(errata E-4, page 59).
