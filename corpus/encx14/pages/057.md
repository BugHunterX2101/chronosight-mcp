## 57.2 E-1: OTP burn requires VDD >= 3.2 V

Description: on rev 0xA1 an OTP burn at VDD below 3.2 V can program some
bits incompletely. ZPOS and MPOS then read back with random errors.

Workaround: burn OTP only at VDD between 3.2 V and 3.6 V (page 36, page
46). Corrected in rev 0xA2.

## 57.3 E-2: MAGH glitch after power-on

Description: on rev 0xA1, STATUS.MAGH can read 1 for up to 1 ms after a
power-on reset even when the field is correct.

Workaround: ignore MAGH for 2 ms after any reset (page 52). Corrected in
rev 0xA2.

## 57.4 How to read the errata

E-3 is the erratum with the largest effect on servo applications. It is
not a silicon defect that a later revision can fix; it is a property of
the reset design that revision 1.0 of this manual described incorrectly.
Every application must apply the E-3 workaround.
