## 14.1 Transfer durations

A 3-byte burst read from STATUS (page 40) consists of the write address
byte, the register pointer byte, a repeated START, the read address byte
and 3 data bytes: 6 bytes with acknowledgements, 54 clock cycles plus the
START, repeated START and STOP conditions.

  Bus speed     Burst read time
  100 kHz       540 µs
  400 kHz       135 µs
  1 MHz         54 µs

## 14.2 Clock stretching

The device never stretches the clock. The host may use any clock frequency
up to the limit of the selected mode.

## 14.3 General call

The device ignores the general call address (0x00).
