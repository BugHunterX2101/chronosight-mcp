# 54 Watchdog and bus faults

## 54.1 Watchdog

With CONF2.WD = 1 the device resets itself if the internal oscillator
stalls for more than 1 ms. The watchdog reset has the same effect as every
other reset: CONF2 = 0x00 and STATUS.RST = 1. Because WD lives in CONF2,
the watchdog is disabled again after the reset until the host rewrites
CONF2.

## 54.2 Bus faults

  Symptom                    Likely cause                     Action
  NACK on address            device in reset or not powered   retry next cycle
  NACK on data               soft reset write (expected)      none
  SDA held low               interrupted transaction          bus recovery
  Wrong data after 0x0F      burst ran past 0x0F (E-4)        limit burst
  Repeated NACK              wiring, ADDR pin, supply         stop, report

Bus recovery is described on page 42.

## 54.3 Host-side timeout

The host should treat more than three consecutive failed reads as an
encoder fault and bring the joint to a safe state. A single NACK is normal
after a reset and must not trip the joint.

## 54.4 After a bus fault

After any bus fault that lasted longer than 1.5 ms, read STATUS and check
RST before using the angle. The fault may have been a reset.
