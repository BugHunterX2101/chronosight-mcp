## 16.1 Hall array position

The Hall array is centred in the body in both x and y within ±0.10 mm, and
sits 0.30 mm below the top surface of the mould compound. Add 0.30 mm to the
mechanical air gap to obtain the magnetic air gap.

## 16.2 Recommended land pattern

  Pad width 0.60 mm, pad length 1.55 mm, pad row pitch 5.40 mm,
  pad pitch 1.27 mm.

Keep the area under the package free of copper pours on the top layer to
avoid eddy-current effects at high rotational speed.
